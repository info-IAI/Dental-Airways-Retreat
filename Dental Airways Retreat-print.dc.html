Who should attend

Written for dentists — beginner or years in.

The retreat is built for practicing dentists. You do not need a sleep background to start; you do need patients whose faces, sleep and breathing you have wondered about.

General dentists

Adding airway screening to the exams you already do every day.

Pediatric dentists

Catching growth and breathing patterns early, while they still respond.

Restorative & prosthodontic dentists

Seeing wear, bruxism and failing restorations as airway findings.

Airway-focused dentists

Deepening case selection, appliance choice and medical collaboration.

Course information

What we will work through together.

Curriculum in development — the four tracks below are placeholders to replace with your final modules.

01

Seeing the airway

Anatomy, growth and the facial signs that walk past you every day. Screening that fits inside a normal hygiene appointment.

02

Imaging & assessment

Reading what you already capture, plus the questionnaires and referrals that turn a hunch into a documented finding.

03

Treatment & collaboration

Appliance selection, myofunctional and surgical partners, and how to write the letter that makes a physician say yes.

04

Monday morning

Case reviews, team scripting and a written protocol you leave with.
